Human-in-the-loop: the life of an approval

With toolApproval, a risky tool call doesn't run. The request ends with an approval request, the human answers (maybe hours later), and a new request resumes from the saved messages. Try a refund (always needs the billing team), a rollback (auto-approved by policy), and a retried POST.
