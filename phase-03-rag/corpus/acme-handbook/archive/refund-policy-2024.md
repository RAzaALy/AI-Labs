# Refund policy (2024)

This page describes the refund policy that applied in 2024. It has been replaced by the current billing and refunds page.

## Refund window

Customers could request a refund within 14 days of a charge. Refunds were processed within 10 business days and required approval from a finance manager.

## Duplicate charges

Duplicate charges were refunded manually by the finance team within one week of the customer reporting them.
