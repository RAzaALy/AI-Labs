# Billing and refunds

## Plans and prices

| Plan | Monthly price | Seats included | Extra seat |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | USD 29 | 3 | USD 10 |
| Team | USD 99 | 10 | USD 9 |
| Business | USD 399 | 50 | USD 8 |
| Enterprise | Custom contract | Custom | Custom |

Annual billing gets two months free. Prices exclude taxes; VAT or sales tax is added based on the billing address.

## Refund policy

Customers can request a full refund within 30 days of a charge if they are not satisfied. After 30 days, refunds are given only for billing errors. Approved refunds are processed within 5 business days and go back to the original payment method. The billing team approves refunds; support agents cannot approve them alone.

## Duplicate charges

If a customer was charged twice for the same invoice, the duplicate is refunded automatically within 24 hours once billing confirms it. Support should look up the invoice id (format `INV-` followed by eight digits, for example `INV-20260412`) and attach it to the ticket.

## Failed payments

When a payment fails, Acme retries the card on day 3, day 5 and day 7. After the third failure the workspace becomes read-only, and after 30 days of non-payment it is suspended. Data in suspended workspaces is kept for 90 days before deletion.

## Changing plans

Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated to the day. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period, so customers keep paid features until then.
