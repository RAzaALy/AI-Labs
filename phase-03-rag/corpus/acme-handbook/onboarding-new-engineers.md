# Onboarding new engineers

Welcome to Acme Cloud. This guide covers your first two weeks.

## Before day one

IT ships a laptop to your home address. On day one you will receive SSO credentials and must set up multi-factor authentication before accessing any system. Your manager assigns you an onboarding buddy from your team.

## Week one

- Set up the development environment with `acme dev setup`, which installs the toolchain and starts local services in Docker.
- Read the architecture overview and the incident response guide.
- Ship a small change to production by Friday of your first week, with your buddy reviewing the pull request. Shipping early builds confidence and tests that your access works.

## Week two

Shadow the on-call engineer for two days to learn the alerting and runbooks. You join the on-call rotation yourself only after three months, and always as a secondary for your first rotation.

## Who to ask

Your buddy is the first person to ask about anything. For access problems contact IT in `#it-help`; for questions about the codebase ask in your team channel. There are no stupid questions in your first month.
